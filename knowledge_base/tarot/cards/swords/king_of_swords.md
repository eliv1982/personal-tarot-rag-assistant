## Core Themes
- authority of mind, reason, and judgment
- strategy, expertise, and ethical decision-making
- law, structure, and formal communication
- objectivity and principled leadership
- the risk of intellect becoming domination

## Upright Meaning
The King of Swords suggests mature judgment, clear standards, and disciplined thinking. It can point to the need for expertise, structure, fairness, or a decision based on evidence rather than emotional pressure.

As a stance, this card asks the user to think strategically and communicate with integrity.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the King of Swords is domination, intimidation, cynicism, rigid logic, or using status and language to control. Reversed, it can show biased judgment, misuse of authority, harsh words, or a decision-making process that lacks fairness.

## Interpretation Notes
- In advice positions, seek facts, expertise, and principled clarity.
- In formal matters, use this card as a prompt for documentation and qualified advice, not prediction.
- In relationship readings, notice whether reason is serving connection or avoiding vulnerability.
- In obstacle positions, power may be expressed through words, rules, or intellectual superiority.

## Reading Cautions
- A court card may describe a role, quality, or dynamic, not only a literal person.
- Do not predict legal outcomes or claim another person's hidden motives as facts.
- If a real person interpretation is possible, keep it tentative and context-based.
