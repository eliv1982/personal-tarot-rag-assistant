## Core Themes
- craft, collaboration, and skilled work
- learning through practice and feedback
- recognition earned through competence
- building something with others
- standards, contribution, and useful expertise

## Upright Meaning
The Three of Pentacles suggests progress through skill, effort, and collaboration. It can point to work, study, craft, apprenticeship, feedback, or a project that improves when people contribute their strengths.

This card values competence and visible effort. It supports building something real, but it does not guarantee promotion, grades, contracts, or public recognition.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the Three of Pentacles is poor workmanship, lack of coordination, unclear standards, under-recognition, or focusing more on approval than the work itself. Reversed, it can show missing skills, weak collaboration, boredom, criticism, or a need to learn before claiming mastery.

## Interpretation Notes
- In work readings, examine roles, feedback, quality, and whether the structure supports good work.
- In study readings, it supports practice, mentorship, and skill-building rather than instant results.
- In relationship readings, it can show practical cooperation or the need to build trust through action.
- In obstacle positions, the issue may be poor coordination, weak effort, or unclear expectations.

## Reading Cautions
- Do not promise promotion, money, diploma, contract, marriage, or legal outcome.
- Do not treat competence as guaranteed success without context.
- Practical/material themes must stay contextual and agency-preserving.
