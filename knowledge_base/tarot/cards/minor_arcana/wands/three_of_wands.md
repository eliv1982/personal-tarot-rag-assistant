## Core Themes
- expansion, momentum, and early results
- collaboration, outreach, and broader horizons
- watching a project begin to take shape
- strategic growth after initial action
- confidence balanced with continued work

## Upright Meaning
The Three of Wands suggests that energy has moved beyond the starting point. The user may be seeing early results, exploring wider possibilities, collaborating, or preparing for the next phase of a project.

This card is encouraging, but it does not mean the work is finished. It asks how to support growth without assuming success is already secured.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the Three of Wands is overconfidence, unrealistic plans, weak collaboration, waiting for help that does not arrive, or disappointment when early promise develops slowly. Reversed, it can show delays, stalled expansion, poor teamwork, blocked opportunities, or a need to revise the plan.

## Interpretation Notes
- In work readings, focus on collaboration, next steps, scope, and realistic support.
- In creative readings, it can indicate a project moving outward toward audience, feedback, or distribution.
- In relationship readings, it may point to shared future-thinking or distance between desire and execution.
- In obstacle positions, the user may need better partners, clearer goals, or more patience.

## Reading Cautions
- Do not promise business success, new work, travel, recognition, or outside help.
- Do not claim another person will support or disappoint the user as fact.
- Energy/action themes must stay contextual and agency-preserving.
