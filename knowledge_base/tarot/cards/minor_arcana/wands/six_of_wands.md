## Core Themes
- recognition, confidence, and visible progress
- encouragement after effort
- public response, morale, and leadership
- the desire to be seen and respected
- success as a moment, not a permanent identity

## Upright Meaning
The Six of Wands suggests visible progress or the possibility of recognition. The user may be emerging from a challenge with more confidence, support, or evidence that their efforts matter.

This card is encouraging, but it should not be read as guaranteed victory. It asks how recognition can be received with humility and used responsibly.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the Six of Wands is pride, dependence on applause, comparison, or measuring worth only through public approval. Reversed, it can show delayed recognition, feeling unseen, communication breakdown, loss of confidence, or a result that does not match expectations.

## Interpretation Notes
- In career readings, it can indicate morale, reputation, visibility, or progress, not a guaranteed promotion.
- In relationship readings, focus on respect, validation, and whether both people feel valued.
- In advice positions, accept encouragement while staying grounded in the work.
- In obstacle positions, the need to win may be distorting the situation.

## Reading Cautions
- Do not promise victory, recognition, promotion, agreement, or successful application.
- Do not claim betrayal, infidelity, or disrespect from another person as fact.
- Energy/action themes must stay contextual and agency-preserving.
