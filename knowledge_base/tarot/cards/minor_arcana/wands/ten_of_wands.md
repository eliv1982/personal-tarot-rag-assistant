## Core Themes
- burden, responsibility, and overextension
- ambition becoming pressure
- burnout, duty, and carrying too much alone
- the cost of success or control
- delegation, limits, and sustainable effort

## Upright Meaning
The Ten of Wands suggests a heavy load. The user may be carrying responsibilities, ambition, work, expectations, or pressure that has become difficult to sustain.

This card does not erase achievement, but it asks whether the current approach is costing too much. The task may need boundaries, delegation, or a more humane pace.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the Ten of Wands is martyrdom, control, overwork, resentment, or believing everything will collapse unless the user carries it alone. Reversed, it can show release of pressure, redistribution of responsibility, collapse from overload, or resistance to asking for help.

## Interpretation Notes
- In work readings, focus on workload, responsibility, burnout risk, and delegation.
- In relationship readings, it can show one person carrying too much emotional or practical labor.
- In advice positions, identify what can be put down, shared, postponed, or simplified.
- In obstacle positions, ambition may have become self-punishment.

## Reading Cautions
- Do not give health certainty or predict specific physical problems.
- Do not claim another person is exploiting the user as fact.
- Energy/action themes must stay contextual, stabilizing, and agency-preserving.
