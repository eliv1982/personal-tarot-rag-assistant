## Core Themes
- movement, adventure, and bold action
- creative momentum and restless ambition
- change, travel, and pursuit of new experience
- charisma, confidence, and challenge
- the tension between passion and consistency

## Upright Meaning
The Knight of Wands suggests active movement and a desire to pursue something with heat and confidence. It can point to a project, journey, relocation, creative push, or energetic shift.

As a role or stance, this card asks the user to engage courageously while staying aware of pacing and follow-through.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the Knight of Wands is impatience, volatility, inconsistency, impulsive desire, or chasing novelty without commitment. Reversed, it can show blocked movement, scattered ambition, delays, conflict, or energy spent in all directions at once.

## Interpretation Notes
- In advice positions, move with courage, but avoid rushing past important details.
- In relationship readings, it can highlight passion and attraction without guaranteeing stability.
- In work or creative readings, it supports momentum, travel, launch energy, or bold experimentation.
- In obstacle positions, speed may be masking avoidance or lack of commitment.

## Reading Cautions
- A court card may describe a role, quality, or dynamic, not only a literal person.
- Do not claim someone is unfaithful, exploitative, or only seeking novelty as fact.
- If a real person interpretation is possible, keep it tentative and context-based.
