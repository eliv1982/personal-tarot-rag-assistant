## Core Themes
- planning, anticipation, and early expansion
- choosing a direction after the first spark
- ambition, partnership, and strategic waiting
- preparing for movement without rushing
- the tension between vision and action

## Upright Meaning
The Two of Wands suggests a planning stage. Something has begun, but the next step requires perspective, preparation, and a decision about where to direct energy.

This card carries ambition and possibility, but it also asks for timing. Not every opportunity is ready for immediate action.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the Two of Wands is overplanning, stalled momentum, pride, fear of leaving the familiar, or relying on potential without committing to a path. Reversed, it can show delays, uncertain partnership, lost enthusiasm, poor planning, or a need to reassess direction.

## Interpretation Notes
- In work or project readings, focus on planning, negotiation, and readiness.
- In relationship readings, it can show shared possibilities or uncertainty about the next step.
- In advice positions, look outward, define the goal, and choose where effort should go.
- In obstacle positions, ambition may be blocked by hesitation, pride, or unclear agreements.

## Reading Cautions
- Do not promise relocation, travel, contract success, promotion, or project success.
- Do not claim another person's intention or commitment as fact.
- Energy/action themes must stay contextual and agency-preserving.
