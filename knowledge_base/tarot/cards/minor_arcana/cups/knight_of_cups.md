## Core Themes
- emotional pursuit, romance, and invitation
- idealism, imagination, and charm
- following a feeling or dream
- offers, proposals, and relational movement
- the tension between sincerity and fantasy

## Upright Meaning
The Knight of Cups suggests movement led by feeling. It can describe an offer, invitation, romantic gesture, creative pursuit, or emotionally meaningful opportunity.

As a pattern, it asks the user to notice what is being pursued: a real connection, a dream, an aesthetic ideal, or a feeling that may need grounding.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the Knight of Cups is emotional inconsistency, seduction without follow-through, passive idealism, avoidance of reality, or promises shaped more by fantasy than commitment. Reversed, it can point to unclear offers, mood-driven choices, disappointment, or the need to verify what is being presented.

## Interpretation Notes
- In relationship readings, this card can indicate romantic movement, but not a guaranteed sincere proposal.
- In advice positions, follow inspiration while checking facts and boundaries.
- In obstacle positions, charm, fantasy, or emotional passivity may be clouding judgment.
- It can describe the stance of approaching gently rather than forcing an outcome.

## Reading Cautions
- A court card may describe a role, quality, or dynamic, not only a literal person.
- Do not claim deception, love, seduction, or hidden intent as fact.
- If a real person interpretation is possible, keep it tentative and context-based.
