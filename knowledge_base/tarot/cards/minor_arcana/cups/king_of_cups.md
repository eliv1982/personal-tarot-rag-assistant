## Core Themes
- emotional maturity, steadiness, and compassion
- wise counsel and calm support
- feeling held with responsibility
- diplomacy, care, and trustworthy presence
- balancing depth of emotion with self-command

## Upright Meaning
The King of Cups suggests emotional steadiness and wise response. It can describe the capacity to remain compassionate without being swept away by feeling.

As a role or stance, this card encourages measured care, good listening, and responsible handling of sensitive emotions.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the King of Cups is emotional control used to avoid vulnerability, charm used to influence, or calmness that hides avoidance. Reversed, it can point to unreliable advice, emotional manipulation, evasiveness, or difficulty acting with emotional integrity.

## Interpretation Notes
- In advice positions, respond with calm, compassion, and mature boundaries.
- In relationship readings, look for emotional availability, trust, and whether care is matched by accountability.
- In professional or family readings, it can suggest counsel, mediation, or support work.
- In obstacle positions, ask whether emotional influence is being used responsibly.

## Reading Cautions
- A court card may describe a role, quality, or dynamic, not only a literal person.
- Do not claim manipulation, exploitation, addiction, or hidden motives as fact.
- If a real person interpretation is possible, keep it tentative and context-based.
