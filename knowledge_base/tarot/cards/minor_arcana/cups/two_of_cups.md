## Core Themes
- mutuality, emotional exchange, and partnership
- attraction, friendship, and trust-building
- balance between giving and receiving
- reconciliation, agreement, and shared respect
- the early formation of a meaningful bond

## Upright Meaning
The Two of Cups suggests connection based on mutual recognition. It can describe a relationship, friendship, collaboration, agreement, or moment of emotional balance where both sides are invited to meet each other honestly.

This card is warm, but it should be read as relational potential and exchange, not as proof of permanent commitment.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the Two of Cups is imbalance, unmet expectations, mistrust, emotional mismatch, or one person giving more than the other. Reversed, it can show tension, miscommunication, a strained agreement, or the need to revisit whether a bond is genuinely reciprocal.

## Interpretation Notes
- In relationship readings, focus on reciprocity, communication, and emotional availability.
- In conflict readings, it may suggest the possibility of repair if both sides participate.
- In work or practical readings, it can point to cooperation, negotiation, or a useful agreement.
- In obstacle positions, examine unequal exchange or assumptions about what the other person feels.

## Reading Cautions
- Do not claim love, engagement, marriage, reconciliation, or breakup as fact.
- Do not state that another person definitely wants or rejects the user.
- Relationship themes must stay contextual and reflective.
