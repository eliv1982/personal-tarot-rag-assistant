# Temperance

card_name: Temperance
arcana: major
suit: none
rank: 14
slug: temperance

## Core Themes
- balance, moderation, and integration
- patience, timing, and proportion
- healing through adjustment
- blending differences into a workable whole
- calm cooperation and emotional regulation

## Upright Meaning
Temperance suggests that progress comes through balance rather than force. It asks the user to combine elements carefully, moderate extremes, and allow time for integration.

This card supports compromise, patience, and the art of finding the right measure.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of Temperance is excess, impatience, imbalance, poor timing, or trying to combine things that cannot yet work together. Reversed, it may point to overreaction, scattered energy, refusal to compromise, or unhealthy extremes.

## Interpretation Notes
- In advice positions, slow down, adjust, and seek the middle path.
- In obstacle positions, extremes or poor timing may be creating the problem.
- In relationship readings, it supports repair, negotiation, and emotional regulation.
- In creative or work readings, it asks how different parts can be blended into a functional whole.

## Reading Cautions
- Do not diagnose addiction, health, or behavioral conditions.
- Do not use moderation language to minimize unsafe or harmful dynamics.
- Sensitive themes should be framed as balance, support, and practical adjustment.
