# Wheel of Fortune

card_name: Wheel of Fortune
arcana: major
suit: none
rank: 10
slug: wheel_of_fortune

## Core Themes
- cycles, change, timing, and turning points
- uncertainty and shifting circumstances
- patterns that repeat until they are understood
- opportunity, disruption, and adaptation
- humility before what cannot be fully controlled

## Upright Meaning
The Wheel of Fortune suggests that the situation is in motion. Conditions may be changing, and the user may need to adapt rather than force a fixed plan.

This card points to cycles and timing. It asks what pattern is turning, what is ending, and where a new opening may appear.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the Wheel is fatalism, passivity, clinging to control, or repeating the same pattern without learning from it. Reversed, it can show delays, resistance to change, unstable timing, or frustration when events do not move as expected.

## Interpretation Notes
- In advice positions, watch the pattern and respond flexibly.
- In obstacle positions, the problem may be resistance to change or over-attachment to certainty.
- In outcome positions, frame change as a possible direction, not destiny.
- This card can highlight timing, but should not be used for rigid prediction.

## Reading Cautions
- Do not claim events are fated or outside all agency.
- Do not encourage gambling, risk-taking, or passive waiting.
- Keep uncertainty explicit and practical.
