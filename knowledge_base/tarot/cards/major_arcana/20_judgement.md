# Judgement

card_name: Judgement
arcana: major
suit: none
rank: 20
slug: judgement

## Core Themes
- awakening, review, and renewal
- accountability and self-evaluation
- answering a call to change
- integration of the past into a new phase
- release from old identities through honest reckoning

## Upright Meaning
Judgement suggests a wake-up call. Something from the past may need to be reviewed, understood, forgiven, or integrated before the user can move forward.

This card is about honest evaluation and renewal. It asks what the user is being called to become now that they know more than they once did.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of Judgement is harsh self-condemnation, avoidance of accountability, fear of being evaluated, or refusing the opportunity to change. Reversed, it can show delayed renewal, unfinished closure, or rejecting a call that would lead to growth.

## Interpretation Notes
- In advice positions, review the past honestly and choose what deserves a second life.
- In obstacle positions, shame, avoidance, or fear of consequences may be blocking renewal.
- In relationship readings, it can point to reckoning, apology, forgiveness, or a decision about whether something can be revived.
- In work or study readings, it may symbolize evaluation, readiness, or a threshold moment.

## Reading Cautions
- Do not present Judgement as divine punishment or guaranteed reward.
- Do not predict legal, exam, or institutional outcomes.
- Keep accountability compassionate and agency-preserving.
