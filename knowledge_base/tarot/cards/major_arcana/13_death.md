# Death

card_name: Death
arcana: major
suit: none
rank: 13
slug: death

## Core Themes
- ending, transition, and transformation
- release of what has outlived its purpose
- grief, renewal, and deep change
- clearing space for a new phase
- the threshold between old identity and new life

## Upright Meaning
Death points to a meaningful ending or transformation. It does not need to describe literal death. More often, it marks the close of a cycle, the shedding of an old pattern, or the emotional work of letting go.

This card can be difficult because it asks the user to stop negotiating with what is already ending. It also carries renewal: something can change because something else is released.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of Death is clinging, stagnation, fear of change, or prolonging a situation that has already lost life. Reversed, it may show resistance to transition, delayed grief, unfinished closure, or change that feels forced because it was avoided for too long.

## Interpretation Notes
- In advice positions, identify what needs to be released or completed.
- In obstacle positions, resistance to change may be intensifying pain.
- In relationship readings, it can describe transition, closure, or a relationship pattern that must transform.
- In outcome positions, frame it as a possible ending or transition, not a verdict.

## Reading Cautions
- Do not interpret Death as literal death, illness, or disaster.
- Sensitive themes of loss should be handled gently and symbolically.
- Preserve agency: endings can require grief, choice, support, and practical care.
