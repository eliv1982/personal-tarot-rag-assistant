## Core Themes
- material mastery, stability, and stewardship
- practical leadership and long-term responsibility
- resources, work, property, and tangible results
- generosity, discipline, and grounded success
- the risk of control, materialism, or status fixation

## Upright Meaning
The King of Pentacles suggests mature practical authority. It can point to stability, careful resource management, business sense, or the ability to build something durable over time.

As a role or stance, this card asks the user to act with discipline, generosity, and respect for material reality.

## Shadow Or Reversed Nuance
The shadow of the King of Pentacles is materialism, greed, rigidity, status anxiety, or using resources as a form of control. Reversed, it can show poor stewardship, financial fear, unethical shortcuts, overwork, or confusing security with possession.

## Interpretation Notes
- In advice positions, make the practical plan solid and sustainable.
- In work or financial readings, focus on stewardship, ethics, long-term value, and grounded decision-making.
- In relationship readings, examine reliability, generosity, control, and shared material priorities.
- In obstacle positions, attachment to security may be limiting growth or care.

## Reading Cautions
- A court card may describe a role, quality, or dynamic, not only a literal person.
- Do not promise wealth, promotion, property success, or financial rescue as fact.
- If a real person interpretation is possible, keep it tentative and context-based.
