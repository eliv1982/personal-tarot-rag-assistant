# Three Card: Relationships

spread_id: three_card_relationships
category: relationships
recommended_use: Understanding each side of a relationship or interaction and the pattern currently active between them.
number_of_cards: 3

## Positions
1. Self: your stance, needs, or contribution in this relationship.
2. Other person: their visible stance, needs, or contribution.
3. Dynamic: how the interaction works now and what pattern is active.

## Interpretation Notes
- Read positions 1 and 2 as perspectives or roles in the dynamic, not as complete portraits of either person.
- Position 3 is the synthesis: it shows the active pattern between the two sides.
- Focus on communication, emotional climate, boundaries, expectations, and what the user can influence.

## Caveats
- Do not claim certainty about another person's hidden thoughts, motives, or actions.
- If the other-person card is unclear, frame it as a visible dynamic or possible emotional stance.
- Preserve user agency and avoid telling the user what the other person will definitely do.
