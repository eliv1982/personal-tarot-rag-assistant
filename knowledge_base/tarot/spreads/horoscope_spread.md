# Horoscope Spread

spread_id: horoscope_spread
category: whole_life_overview
recommended_use: Broad overview of many life areas, especially when the user wants a wide map rather than a narrow answer.
number_of_cards: 12 or 13

## Positions
1. Self and vitality: identity, body, personal presence, beginnings.
2. Resources and values: money, possessions, stability, priorities.
3. Communication and learning: siblings, neighbors, short trips, study, messages, daily information.
4. Home and roots: family, home, foundations, emotional security.
5. Creativity and pleasure: children, romance, hobbies, play, self-expression.
6. Work and routines: duties, service, daily work, habits, care of the body.
7. Partnerships: committed relationships, contracts, close allies, open opposition.
8. Shared resources and transformation: intimacy, debts, inheritances, other people's resources, deep change.
9. Beliefs and expansion: travel, higher learning, philosophy, publishing, wider perspective.
10. Career and public role: profession, reputation, status, responsibility, long-term direction.
11. Friends and hopes: groups, networks, community, aspirations, shared causes.
12. Inner life and hidden matters: solitude, retreat, secrets, unconscious patterns, spiritual reflection.
13. Optional center card: overall theme or integrating message for the spread.

## Interpretation Notes
- This spread works as a map of life areas. Each card should be interpreted through its house position.
- If a center card is used, read it first as the main theme or last as the synthesis.
- Look for clusters: repeated suits, strong major arcana, or several difficult cards in related houses.
- Keep sensitive houses symbolic and grounded. For example, health, death, legal, or financial themes should be framed as areas of reflection or practical attention, not predictions.

## Caveats
- This is a broad spread and can become too diffuse for a specific question.
- Use it when the user wants an overview, not when they need focused guidance on one issue.
- Because some houses touch sensitive topics, avoid factual claims about illness, death, legal outcomes, pregnancy, betrayal, or financial collapse.
