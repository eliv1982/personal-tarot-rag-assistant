# Three Card: Past, Present, Future

spread_id: three_card_past_present_future
category: timeline_reflection
recommended_use: Simple timeline reading for understanding how a past influence connects to the present and a possible next direction.
number_of_cards: 3

## Positions
1. Past: an earlier influence, pattern, choice, or experience that still shapes the question.
2. Present: the current state, active tension, or central reality of the situation.
3. Future: a likely direction or developing possibility if the current pattern continues.

## Interpretation Notes
- Read the three cards as a movement, not as three isolated definitions.
- The past card does not need to describe a literal event; it may describe an emotional pattern or inherited context.
- The present card is the anchor of the reading. Use it to decide which meanings of the past and future cards are most relevant.
- The future card should be framed as a possible trajectory, not a fixed outcome.

## Caveats
- Avoid treating the future position as fate.
- If the future card is difficult, describe what pattern may lead there and what the user can notice or adjust.
- If the cards point in different directions, name the shift or tension between past, present, and future.
