# One Card

spread_id: one_card
category: focused_reflection
recommended_use: Quick check-in, daily reflection, clarifying a simple question, or identifying the main theme of the moment.
number_of_cards: 1

## Positions
1. Central message: the main theme, focus, or reflective prompt for the current question.

## Interpretation Notes
- Read the card as one symbolic lens, not as a full diagnosis of the situation.
- Connect the card to the user's question and current context.
- Useful questions: What stands out in the image or meaning? What feeling, pattern, or choice does it highlight?
- This spread works well with journaling. Record the card, first impressions, later reflections, and any pattern that becomes clearer over time.
- A clarifier card may be drawn if the first card feels too broad or unclear. The clarifier should refine the first card, not replace it.

## Caveats
- Do not force a single answer from one card if the question is complex.
- For high-stakes or emotionally loaded questions, keep the reading cautious and reflective.
- A one-card reading is best for focus and perspective, not detailed forecasting.
