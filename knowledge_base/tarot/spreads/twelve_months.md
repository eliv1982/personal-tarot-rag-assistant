# Twelve Months

spread_id: twelve_months
category: yearly_overview
recommended_use: Reflective overview of the next twelve months, useful for planning themes, emotional focus, and seasonal patterns.
number_of_cards: 12 or 13

## Positions
1. Current month: the main theme or focus for the present month.
2. Next month: the theme likely to become active after the current month.
3. Month 3: the third monthly theme in sequence.
4. Month 4: the fourth monthly theme in sequence.
5. Month 5: the fifth monthly theme in sequence.
6. Month 6: the sixth monthly theme in sequence.
7. Month 7: the seventh monthly theme in sequence.
8. Month 8: the eighth monthly theme in sequence.
9. Month 9: the ninth monthly theme in sequence.
10. Month 10: the tenth monthly theme in sequence.
11. Month 11: the eleventh monthly theme in sequence.
12. Month 12: the twelfth monthly theme in sequence.
13. Optional overall theme: the larger pattern or integrating message for the year.

## Interpretation Notes
- Read each card as a monthly theme, not as a guaranteed event.
- The spread is useful for noticing cycles, emphasis, and areas that may need attention.
- If the optional thirteenth card is used, treat it as the overall tone or lesson of the year.
- Compare adjacent months for transitions, repeating suits, and shifts in energy.

## Caveats
- Do not use this spread for rigid prediction.
- Long-range readings are naturally uncertain; keep language broad, reflective, and agency-preserving.
- Avoid making high-impact claims about health, legal matters, money, pregnancy, death, betrayal, or other sensitive outcomes.
